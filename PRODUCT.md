# Salsabil Construction — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html` plus `img/`), no frameworks, no tracking; Cal Sans from Google Fonts is the only external resource. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- Homeowners in New York and New Jersey who need renovation or remodeling work, mostly finding the link on a phone inside Facebook's or Instagram's in-app browser.
- The owner (Sam), who receives the result as a text message on (347) 243-9333.

## Purpose
Give a remodeling contractor whose whole public presence is one Facebook group ad and an Instagram account a real website: say what they do and where, and make it one tap to text or call Sam for a quote.

## Positioning
A renovation and remodeling contractor covering six trades under one name: renovations and remodeling, kitchens and bathrooms, flooring and tile, painting and drywall, framing and carpentry, interior and exterior improvements (all from their own flyer). Their own tagline: "Quality Work. Honest Service. Professional Results."
- *Inferred:* the selling point for a multi-trade remodeler is one number and one crew for the whole job. Not stated by the owner.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact by call or text only: "Call or Text Sam" (flyer/ad). No email, no booking link.
- Quote form composes a pre-written text to Sam; fallbacks are "Open in Messages", "Copy message" and the number.
- Service area: "NY & NJ" (flyer). No towns published, so none are named.
- No hours, prices, licence, insurance, years in business, reviews or work photos available. None are claimed.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: black and orange (#F8580A) house mark with hammer and saw over "SALSABIL / CONSTRUCTION" (from the flyer, keyed out of its background).
- Calm band style (paper and navy bands, the logo's orange as the one accent fill, Cal Sans headings).
- Voice: "we" (the business's own caption uses "we"), with Sam named as the person you reach.

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook group ad (2026-10-20 Muse run): caption services and tagline, flyer image.
- Flyer: logo, phone, "NY & NJ", @salsabil_construction, six services.
- Instagram @salsabil_construction: live, "Salsabil Construction", 37 posts, 109 followers (checked 2026-10-20). Posts not fetchable without login.

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen says who, what, where and how to reach Sam.
2. Only real facts; gaps stay gaps.
3. Fewer contact options, each one working.
